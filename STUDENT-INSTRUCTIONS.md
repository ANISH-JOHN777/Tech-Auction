# TECH AUCTION 2026 — STUDENT PARTICIPANT GUIDE

**SNS College of Technology — Department of Information Technology**  
*Welcome to Tech Auction 2026! Read these instructions carefully before starting.*

---

## 1. Login & Accessing the Portal
1. Open the hackathon portal URL in your web browser: `https://tech-auction-2026.vercel.app`
2. Enter your assigned **Team Code** (e.g., `FS01`, `FS02`, `CY01`) and **Team PIN** (default: `1234`).
3. Click **ENTER HACKATHON**.

---

## 2. Track Selection
- After login, choose your assigned track: **Full-Stack Web Development** or **Cybersecurity**.
- **Important**: Track selection is permanent once confirmed. You cannot switch tracks later.

---

## 3. Understanding Virtual Credits & Wallets
- Every team starts with **1,000 virtual auction credits**.
- Your credit balance is displayed in the top right corner of the navigation bar.
- Use your credits during live auction rounds to buy technical hints, extra development time, or AI access.

---

## 4. Live Auction Bidding Rules
- When an auction round begins for your track:
  1. The item name, starting price, and countdown timer will appear in the **Auction Room**.
  2. To bid, click **PLACE BID**. Each bid must meet or exceed the minimum increment requirement.
  3. When you place a bid, your bid amount is temporarily held from your wallet balance.
  4. If another team outbids you, your held credits are instantly refunded back to your available balance.
  5. The team with the highest bid when the countdown timer hits 0 wins the auction item!

---

## 5. Using Purchased Advantages & AI ASSIST
- **Hints & Time Credits**: Unlocked hints will appear directly in your challenge workspace. Extra time credits automatically extend your team's deadline.
- **AI ASSIST**:
  - If your team wins the `AI ASSIST` item, an **Activate AI Assist** button will unlock on your dashboard.
  - Clicking **Activate AI Assist** starts a **15-minute countdown timer**.
  - During this window, your team can submit up to **30 questions** to the AI assistant for guidance on debugging, SQL queries, or security concepts.

---

## 6. Working on Challenges & Submissions
1. Open your challenge workspace:
   - **Full-Stack**: CampusConnect problem package (Identify and fix bugs in React/Node application).
   - **Cybersecurity**: SecureVault lab package (Identify vulnerabilities and document mitigations).
2. Use **Save Draft** regularly to store your progress.
3. When your solution is ready, click **SUBMIT FINAL SOLUTION**.
4. **Warning**: Once submitted as FINAL, your submission is locked and cannot be edited. Ensure all files and descriptions are complete before submitting!

---

## 7. Submission Deadline & Leaderboard
- All final submissions must be completed before the countdown timer hits 0.
- The official leaderboard will be published by faculty judges after all submissions are evaluated.
